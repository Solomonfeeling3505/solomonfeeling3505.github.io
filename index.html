🤔 What is ATGENX-Klipper-Studio?

ATGENX-Klipper-Studio is a friendly desktop program that helps you create, check, and safely upload configuration files for your 3D printer running Klipper firmware. Whether you're a beginner or an expert, this tool makes managing your printer's settings simple and stress-free.

Think of it as your personal assistant for your 3D printer's brain. It supports 83 different printer boards, so chances are your printer is covered. You don't need to be a programmer to use it – we've designed it to be easy for everyone.

✨ Amazing Features

🛠️ Build Config Files

Create complete printer.cfg files from scratch or use templates. No more typing complicated code by hand!

✅ Check Your Settings

The app automatically checks your configuration for errors and warnings before you upload anything. Avoid costly mistakes!

🔒 Safe Upload

Upload your config safely to your printer via Moonraker. The app ensures your printer won't be damaged by bad settings.

🌍 English & Arabic

Use the app in English or Arabic (Beta). Perfect for Arabic-speaking 3D printing enthusiasts!

🔗 Smart Merge

Combine different configuration parts intelligently without conflicts. Save time and avoid headaches.

🎨 User-Friendly Design

Clean, modern interface that's easy to navigate. You'll feel at home right away, even if you're new to 3D printing.
